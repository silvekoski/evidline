product

## Users and purpose

Plant operators and data analysts investigate undocumented sensor streams and customer data. Evidline connects sensor roles, drift, faults, and knowledge claims to inspectable evidence. Raw process data stays local. These requirements come from README.md and PRD.md sections 2 and 8.

## Brand and design principles

The existing product is restrained, precise, and evidence-led. Preserve the supplied Evidline vector identity, compact shadcn controls, Geist typography, and the current application theme. Keep interactions immediate and conclusions traceable. Avoid decorative controls, unsupported claims, or competing visual systems, as specified in PRD.md.

## Accessibility

PRD.md requires status to remain understandable through words, shapes, and line styles for color-blind operators. The existing interface supports reduced motion; additions must preserve that preference and accessible names.
